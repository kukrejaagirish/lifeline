🚑 Every minute matters between an ambulance and an ICU bed.

I built Life-Line, a live coordination board that puts ambulance crews, hospitals, police, traffic control and a command centre on one shared screen during an emergency transfer.

What it does:
• The crew files a transfer, and Command and the receiving hospital see it instantly on every device
• ICU bed reservation and smart destination suggestions
• AI triage turns field notes into priority, department and equipment
• One-click AI situation report for the commander
• SLA watchdog flags and escalates delayed transfers
• NEW: a Raspberry Pi kit in the ambulance streams GPS, SpO₂ and heart rate. If oxygen drops, the hospital sees the alert before the ambulance arrives.

Under the hood: pure Python + vanilla JavaScript, zero dependencies, 66 automated tests, role-based access with PIN-protected command roles, and English / Hindi / Marathi.
